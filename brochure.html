Let’s Play !! — The Smarter Way to Manage Events

Effortless Event Management for Clubs & Players

Let’s Play !! is a modern web app designed to make organizing, booking, and scoring events simple and enjoyable for everyone. Whether you’re a club administrator or a passionate player, Let’s Play !! streamlines your experience from registration to results.

Key Features

For Players

  • Browse & Book Events: Instantly view upcoming events and secure your spot with a click.
  • Personal Schedule: See all your matches and bookings in one place.
  • Score Entry: Enter or edit your match scores directly from your schedule—no paperwork needed!

For Clubs & Admins

  • Easy Event Creation: Set up new events, courts, and rounds in minutes.
  • Automated Scheduling: Let Let’s Play !! handle team assignments and match scheduling.
  • Real-Time Results: Track scores and standings as soon as matches are completed.
  • System Settings: Manage club preferences and event rules with a user-friendly admin panel.

Smart Scheduling—For Admins & Players

For Admins

  • Flexible Event Setup: Easily configure each event with the number of courts, rounds, and rest periods. Tailor the format to your club’s needs.
  • Rest Time Management: Specify rest times between matches for players, ensuring everyone gets the breaks they need.
  • Wait List Handling: Enable automatic wait lists—when an event fills up, extra players are added to the wait list and notified if a spot opens.
  • Automated Scheduling: Once the event is configured, Let’s Play !! automatically generates the match schedule, assigning players to courts and rounds with optimal rest and fairness.

For Players

  • Personalized Match Schedule: Each player receives a clear, individual schedule showing who they play, on which court, and in which round.
  • Rest Time Visibility: Players can see when their rest periods are scheduled, so they can plan their day and stay refreshed.
  • Wait List Updates: If you’re on the wait list, Let’s Play !! keeps you informed about your status and notifies you if you’re moved into the event.
  • No Surprises: All scheduling is handled automatically, so players always know where to be and when.

How Scheduling Works

  1. Admin sets up the event: Chooses number of courts, rounds, and rest times. Enables wait list if desired.
  2. Let’s Play !! generates the schedule: Assigns matches, courts, and rest periods. Handles wait list automatically.
  3. Players view their schedule: See all matches, opponents, courts, and rest times. Get notified of any changes or wait list updates.
